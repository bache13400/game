How to play

  1. Slide the swamp. Use arrow keys or WASD, swipe the board, or tap the direction buttons.
  2. Match frog twins. Two identical frogs combine into the next evolution. Each frog can merge only once per move.
  3. Grow your pond. Every valid move spawns a tiny frog. Invalid moves don't waste a frog.
  4. Chase the chaos. Discover all 12 evolutions. Reach Ultimate Chaos Frog to win — then keep playing if you dare.

Your board and best score are saved automatically. Press R to restart (you'll be asked first).
